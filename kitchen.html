Our culinary playground

An ever evolving kitchen of creativity and craft. The menu follows the seasons and the artistry of our chefs, with bold flavors and unexpected twists in every bite.

We bake our pretzels from sourdough, fold our empanadas by hand, roll our sausage rolls in puff pastry and make our hummus, dressings and beer cheese in house. Flatbreads come out of the oven to order.

Expect weekly pop ups showcasing global flavors and themed cuisine nights. Every dish tells a story, and the journey never stops.

Menu inspirations

Built with the cellar

Every menu we design starts with what's in our tanks. Bitterness cuts through richness, roast meets char, acidity lifts fat, and carbonation resets the palate between bites. Pick a world to start from and we'll cook toward it.

01

Coastal California

Monterey Bay catch, citrus cured ceviche, charred avocado, grilled fish tacos and whatever the boats brought in that morning.

Pour a bright pale ale. Citrus hops echo the lime, and the bitterness keeps the fat in check.

02

Oaxacan Market

Mole negro, tlayudas, slow braised barbacoa, smoked chile salsas and handmade tortillas still warm from the comal.

Pour a milk stout. Cocoa and roast meet the chocolate and chile in the mole like old friends.

03

Levantine Table

A long table of mezze: hummus, labneh, charred eggplant, za'atar flatbreads, herbed rice and slow roasted lamb with pomegranate.

Pour a fruited mixed fermentation. Tartness lifts the tahini and the fruit plays with pomegranate.

04

Brazilian Churrasco

Fire grilled picanha, linguiça, pão de queijo by the basket, farofa, black beans and a sharp vinaigrette salsa to cut through it all.

Pour a hazy IPA. Soft tropical fruit and a creamy body stand up to salt and smoke.

05

Izakaya Night

Yakitori off the grill, karaage, miso glazed vegetables, quick pickles and rice bowls built for passing around.

Pour a crisp blonde or lager. Clean and quenching, it lets soy, ginger and smoke speak.

06

Smokehouse

Low and slow ribs, brisket and pulled pork, bark you can hear, house pickles, slaw and sides worth fighting over.

Pour a West Coast IPA. Resinous pine and dry bitterness slice straight through the smoke.

07

Tuscan Harvest

Hand rolled pasta, wood fired flatbreads, rosemary roasted vegetables, good olive oil and a slow afternoon.

Pour a farmhouse or blonde ale. Peppery, dry and herbal, made for a table that lingers.

08

Garden & Grain

A fully plant forward spread: seasonal vegetables from the Central Coast, ancient grains, bright herbs and bold dressings.

Pour a fruited sour. Acidity and fruit make vegetables taste like the main event.

These are starting points, not set menus. Our chefs design each one with you, around your guests, your season and your beer.
