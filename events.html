Good to Know

How do we secure our date?

Your date is held once we receive your signed event agreement and a 50% non-refundable deposit. Your final guest count is due seven days before the event.

Can we bring a cake, decorations or vendors?

Yes. Bring your own cake or desserts and decorate your space; just share plans in advance so we can coordinate setup. Outside vendors are considered case by case.

Can you handle dietary needs?

Share preferences and allergies during planning and we'll talk through options. Final menu selections are due seven days before your event.

What if it rains?

Outdoor events go ahead unless conditions are unsafe. If we need to reschedule, we'll find a new date together and your payments transfer over.

What's included in the pricing?

Venue fees cover your space and the event support in your collection. Food and drinks are priced separately, with a 20% service charge on food and beverage. Gratuity is optional.
