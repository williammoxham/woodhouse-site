Our corner of town

Between the river and downtown

We sit where the Westside meets downtown, a block off River Street. The San Lorenzo River runs just to the east, Pacific Avenue, downtown's main strip of shops, bookstores and theaters, is about a seven minute walk, and the redwoods of Pogonip and Harvey West Park start just up the road.

It's an easy stop on the way in or out of town: grab a pint after a ride in the hills, before a show downtown, or on a long afternoon between the beach and the mountains.

Then make a day of it

01

Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk

California's oldest surviving amusement park, on the beach since 1907. The Giant Dipper is a rite of passage.

02

West Cliff Drive

A coastal path along the cliffs from the wharf past Steamer Lane's surfers to Natural Bridges and its rock arch. Grab a snack at Steamer Lane Supply on the way.

03

The Redwoods

Pogonip is right up the road. Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park and its old growth groves are a short drive into the mountains.

04

Downtown

Pacific Avenue's bookstores, record shops, cafés and theaters, about a seven minute walk from our patio. Grab a Vietnamese or Cuban coffee at Mariposa.

05

The Wharf

Walk out over the bay, look for sea lions underneath, and watch the sun go down over Monterey Bay.

06

Back to Woodhouse

Finish with a fresh pint, something from the kitchen and whoever's on our stage tonight.
