A destination

An evolving community space for Santa Cruz. Small batch craft fermentation, and a platform for the boundless intersections of music, art and imagination.

We want to be a major moving part of our community: to bring culture to Santa Cruz, and to be a place where everyone comes together to experience what this beautiful city has to offer.

We do not compete when it comes to community. Woodhouse celebrates other entrepreneurs and creators, makers in every form, from every walk of life.

A quick update on our beer

Santa Cruz drank us dry. When a whole town shows up for a small independent brewery, beer disappears faster than you can make it. Thank you.

We've raised the bar across the brewery and brought on two meticulous, science driven brewers focused on consistency and quality. The early results are outstanding.

While the next batches finish conditioning, you'll see guest taps from breweries we admire. Every pint helps us build something better.

The next generation of Woodhouse beer is on its way.
