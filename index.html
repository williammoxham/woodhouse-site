A destination

An evolving community space for Santa Cruz. Small batch craft fermentation, and a platform for the boundless intersections of music, art and imagination.

We want to be a major moving part of our community: to bring culture to our town, and to be a place where everyone comes together to experience what this beautiful city has to offer.

We do not compete when it comes to community. Woodhouse celebrates other entrepreneurs and creators, makers in every form, from every walk of life.

Why the name

Blending people & culture.

Blending and brewing community, positive relationships and cultural backgrounds. Afrobeats nights, line dancing, reggae, square dances, figure drawing and comedy. Regulars become friends, friends become family, and every background brings something to the table.
